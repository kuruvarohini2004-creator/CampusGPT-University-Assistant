CAMPUSGPT UNIVERSITY KNOWLEDGE BASE

ATTENDANCE:
Students should regularly attend classes.
Students must follow the attendance percentage prescribed
by their university and department.
For attendance shortage, students should contact their
class coordinator.

EXAMINATIONS:
Students should check the university examination timetable.
Hall tickets must be collected before examinations.
Students should carry their university ID card to examinations.

ASSIGNMENTS:
Students should submit assignments before the deadlines.
Students should follow the instructions provided by faculty.
Late submissions are subject to department rules.

PLACEMENTS:
Students should prepare aptitude, programming,
communication and technical interview skills.
Students should regularly check placement announcements.

LIBRARY:
Students can use the university library for academic
books and reference materials.
Students must follow library borrowing and return rules.

ACADEMIC SUPPORT:
Students should contact their faculty members for
subject-related doubts.
Students should consult their department coordinator
for academic concerns.
